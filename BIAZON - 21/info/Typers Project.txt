Kuchinashi Yume (梔子ユメ) is a non-playable character in Blue Archive who was the late president of Abydos High School's student council.

She is a central character in the third chapter of Volume 1.

Introduction
Little is currently known about the final president of Abydos High School's old student council, other than that she was "naive" and a "fool", according to her junior, Takanashi Hoshino.

Despite her position as the student council president, her competence wasn't on par with her title, approaching major problems recklessly without being aware of the consequences her actions might cause.

Personality
Yume was a student who believed and relied on miracles a bit too much, according to Hoshino. Although she had a kind attitude, she was deemed irresponsible and reckless by her own junior; in other words, a fitting way to describe her is that she was blinded by her own hope. Despite everything she went through though, a smile as bright as her hope can always be seen on her face.

In fact, her overly optimistic attitude and excessive kindness led to toxic positivity where she makes reckless decisions and continues on, despite how pointless the efforts are. She is also likely forgetful, as she forgets to bring her compass. She was also very naive that she gets herself into trouble and lets others take advantage of her. She is also against violence, believing that it isn't a good solution and it would eventually become worse. Ultimately, all these traits as a whole eventually led Yume to her own fate.

Appearance
Yume has a cyan long hair reaching her knees, and long cowlick, reaching her neck. She has golden yellow eyes. Band aids are scattered around on to her arms, and legs.

Halo
Yume's halo consists of two yellow rings with the symbol of Abydos in a diamond within the rings.

Uniform
She wears a tucked-in white shirt with a cyan necktie tucked-in into her strap, She also had a tactical belt on both shoulders and a belt around her waist. For legwear, she dons a black plaid skirt, along with a pair of white socks, and a pair of black sneakers with white laces and soles.

swimsuit: She was wearing a dark blue/dark navy one-piece school swimsuit, with the Abydos logo on the right side of her chest. She had three armbands on her right arm and two on her left arm, one of which was white, There are two bandages on the right leg and three bandages on the left leg, one of which is white. She was wearing dark blue flip-flops with the Abydos logo.


Firearm
She used a grey Beretta 92A1 pistol with a silver barrel and black grips.

She also once had a collapsing ballistic shield called the "Iron Horus" that could hold the pistol and several more spare magazines. These items would be kept for safekeeping by Hoshino, where she would use them in her armed variant.

 Shiroko Terror (シロコ＊テラー) is one of the students studying in Abydos High School who wields an Assault Rifle. She is a Nameless Goddess and a member of the Countermeasures Committee. She was also a member of the Iridescent Vanguard.

A version of Shiroko from another world, who came to this place with Phrenapates.

She's the same person in a sense, but with an older aura. Just like Shiroko, she'll do what it takes, no matter how outlandish the idea, for the sake of revitalizing her academy and district. Known as the "God of Death - Anubis" (死の神〜アヌビス), this version of Shiroko was one who had fallen into despair after the loss of her friends and Sensei, subsequently making contact with the Iridescence and becoming the first Iridescent Vanguard member.

Personality
A visibly gloomy version of Shiroko who's most definitely affected by her past and present. Having suffered the loss of her loved ones, she came into the world to fulfill the role of bringing this world to its predetermined end. However, it seems she is still quite hesitant in doing so after knowing the existence of certain people.

Following the events of Volume F, she has taken up residence in the Abydos district as a vigilant eye for her juniors, selling a variety of items abandoned in the district due to desertification for money.[1]

Much like with the current Shiroko, she has taken up cycling once more as a hobby despite initially being reluctant.

Appearance
Shiroko (Terror) has misty-gray, waist-length hair, and a pair of wolf ears, sharing her hair color, as well as having white fluff on the inside. She has cyan eyes with heterochromia, having a white pupil on the left, and a black one on the right with vertical slit pupils. She has pale skin.

Halo
While generally the same as her regular iteration, her halo is black with a slight dark-green glow around the edges with shattered pieces on one section. On the illustration of her playable version, her halo has turned light blue, hinting that her mental state has healed somewhat in part due to Sensei's guidance.

Uniform
She wears a black bustier dress, the skirt parting at her left thigh. The dress is long sleeved, with white cuffs, and a dark blue-green interior. A black choker is found around her neck, and a cyan T-shaped hairpin near her left temple. She wears dark grey high heels with straps, and a holster on her left thigh.

Firearm
She uses the same firearm as her regular iteration, with the exception that hers is repainted mostly black. Most notable is the name: Black Fang 465.

She uses a SIG Sauer P226R in several cutscenes, most notably when she aims her pistol at the Shittim Chest.

Takanashi Hoshino (小鳥遊 ホシノ) is one of the students studying in Abydos High School who wields a Shotgun.

She is the president of the Abydos Student Council, the chairwoman of the Countermeasures Committee, and one of the deuteragonists of Volume 1.

Takanashi Hoshino is the president of the Abydos Student Council, the only registered third-year of Abydos High School, and the current chairwoman for the Countermeasures Commitee.

Personality
Speaking like a senile aunt, she enjoys playing around and napping more than work. Because of this, she usually gets scolded by the rest of the Committee. But, once the mission starts, she protects her comrades by fighting valiantly on the front lines. Since accepting the title of student council president, she has gradually brightened up. Perhaps this is the real Hoshino.

Young: Guarded by a traumatic past, Hoshino’s younger self is characterized as an extremely serious, stern, and unapproachable individual. She is highly straightforward, blunt, and harbors a grumpy temperament, showing absolutely zero tolerance for slackers or nonsense. Deeply scarred and fiercely independent, she possesses an ironclad, stubborn will; she fiercely refuses to rely on others or show even a fraction of vulnerability, believing that exposing weakness invites destruction. She operates with a rigid militaristic mindset, often being cynical and cold, and is fully willing to resort to overwhelming tactical violence to eliminate threats and solve problems immediately, making both allies and enemies deeply wary of her presence.

Appearance
She has pink hair reaching to her legs in the back with a cowlick in the front.

She has heterochromia of blue and orange eyes. She has a small cute little fang.

Young: Hoshino features a noticeably shorter hairstyle than her current self, with her vibrant pastel-pink hair cut in a short bob that frames her face, accentuated by a prominent, twitching cowlick (ahoge) on top. Her hair is tied into two small, low-slung twin-tails near her ears, secured with simple black hair ties instead of her signature whale hairpins. While she possesses the same distinct heterochromia eyes—a bright sky-blue left eye and a golden-yellow right eye—her gaze is completely devoid of its usual sleepy warmth. Instead, her eyes appear dull and sharp, constantly exuding a cold, piercing, and intensely serious aura that keeps everyone at a distance.

Halo
Hoshino's halo has a pink eye-like circle with an outer line curved around and ends with a strip line.

Uniform
Hoshino wears the Abydos school uniform in a relaxed, casual style. She omits the standard blazer entirely, wearing a loose white collared dress shirt with the top buttons left undone and her vibrant cyan-blue tie loosely fastened around her neck. Her Abydos student identification access badge is clipped near her shirt pocket on the left chest. Across her torso, she wears a distinct black tactical shoulder harness with adjustment buckles, paired with a matching black waist belt. Her shirt is neatly tucked into a dark gray pleated plaid skirt featuring a subtle grid pattern. Her hands are covered with fingerless black tactical gloves. For her footwear, she wears an asymmetrical pair of white socks, with the left side being a standard ribbed sock and the right side being a loose ankle sock, completed with navy blue high-top sneakers with white laces and soles.

Gym: She was wearing a white top with light blue trim, the collar pulled to the right revealing her collarbone. Tight-fitting black athletic short. Wear a small white cloth on she right hand.

Young: For her young version, Hoshino wears a neat and strictly fastened Abydos school uniform. It consists of a crisp white collared dress shirt with a vibrant cyan-blue tie properly tightened around her neck. Over her shirt, she wears a heavily detailed black tactical bulletproof vest featuring multiple utility pockets, a prominent pistol holster mounted in the middle of the chest, and a red-and-white striped insignia tag on the left chest. Her right upper arm is fitted with a wide black armband displaying the Abydos logo. She pairs this with a dark gray pleated plaid skirt with a subtle grid pattern. Her hands are covered with fingerless black tactical gloves. On her legs, she wears a symmetrical pair of plain white knee-high socks, completed with sturdy black tactical boots
