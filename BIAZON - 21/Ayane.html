Okusora Ayane

Okusora Ayane (奥空アヤネ) is one of the students studying in Abydos High School who wields a Handgun.

She is a member of the Countermeasures Committee.

INTRODUCTION

A serious secretary of Abydos' Countermeasures Committee. The type of girl who values principles and rules, she's doing her earnest to restore Abydos High.

Personality

A shy and patient girl who displays great tolerance for her committee members' often wacky ideas and plans. However, once her patience has been exhausted, she's been known to throw a tantrum of epic proportions.

Appearance

She has black bob cut hair with bangs and a French-braid styled headband that matches her hair color. She
wears red glasses, white butterfly hairpin with red stud and a speaker earpiece. She has elven ears and amber eyes. Ayane's halo has a red crosshair inside on another circle with holes on the oval part on the crosshair.

Uniform (+Idol Uniform)

She wears a standard uniform, including a navy blue blazer with Abydos access badge, cuffed sleeves, a beige sweater, a tucked-in white shirt and cyan tie underneath, and a pleated, plaid black skirt.

She wears a cyan armband with dark stripes, with kanji written in it. She has a black socks with a butterfly on the rim and brown uwabaki with red shoelaces.

Gym ver: She was wearing a gym shirt with blue cuffs and hem. There was a gym jacket similar to serika. She was wearing athletic underwear with white trim on both sides of her hips. She wore calf-length white socks and black and white sneakers with a cyan-colored midsole.

Idol version: She wore a black officer's cap with a white stripe, and on her right ear, a headset with a microphone. Around her neck, she wore a black leather choker with a buckle and a necklace with a small red pearl. She was no longer wearing glasses. She wore a black sleeveless top with a
zipper revealing a gray bandeau top underneath. She wore black shorts with a white belt, two beige straps on either side of the waist, and a long fabric panel at the back. On her right thigh, she wore a black thigh-high belt. She wore asymmetrical black boots; the right boot extended past her knee,
the left boot extended to her calf, revealing dark gray stockings with white soles. On her hands, she wore fingerless gloves revealing fingers painted black, and black netting on her arms.

Firearm

She uses a white SIG Sauer P229 pistol with a black grip and a red trigger. A red tassel with a white butterfly accessory is attached below the grip. The Abydos insignia is imprinted on the left side of the slide.
