Shiroko Terror

Shiroko Terror (シロコ＊テラー) is one of the students studying in Abydos High School who wields an Assault Rifle. She is a Nameless Goddess and a member of the Countermeasures Committee. She was also a member of the Iridescent Vanguard.
A version of Shiroko from another world, who came to this place with Phrenapates.

INTRODUCTION

She's the same person in a sense, but with an older aura. Just like Shiroko, she'll do what it takes, no matter how outlandish the idea, for the sake of revitalizing her academy and district. Known as the "God of Death - Anubis" (死の神〜アヌビス), this version of Shiroko was one who had fallen into despair after the loss of her friends and Sensei, subsequently making contact with the Iridescence and becoming the first Iridescent Vanguard member.

Personality

A visibly gloomy version of Shiroko who's most definitely affected by her past and present. Having suffered the loss of her loved ones, she came into the world to fulfill the role of bringing this world to its predetermined end. However, it seems she is still quite hesitant in doing so after knowing the existence of certain people.
Following the events of Volume F, she has taken up residence in the Abydos district as a vigilant eye for her juniors, selling a variety of items abandoned in the district due to desertification for money.

Much like with the current Shiroko, she has taken up cycling once more as a hobby despite initially being reluctant.

Appearance

Shiroko (Terror) has misty-gray, waist-length hair, and a pair of wolf ears, sharing her hair color, as well as having white fluff on the inside. She has cyan eyes with heterochromia, having a white pupil on the left, and a black one on the right with vertical slit pupils. She has pale skin. While generally the same as her regular iteration, her halo is black with a slight dark-green glow around the edges with shattered pieces on one section. On the illustration of her playable version, her halo has turned light blue, hinting that her mental state has healed somewhat in part due to Sensei's guidance.

Uniform

She wears a black bustier dress, the skirt parting at her left thigh. The dress is long sleeved, with white cuffs, and a dark blue-green interior. A black choker is found around her neck, and a cyan T-shaped hairpin near her left temple. She wears dark grey high heels with straps, and a holster on her left thigh.

Firearm

She uses the same firearm as her regular iteration, with the exception that hers is repainted mostly black. Most notable is the name: Black Fang 465. She uses a SIG Sauer P226R in several cutscenes, most notably when she aims her pistol at the Shittim Chest.
