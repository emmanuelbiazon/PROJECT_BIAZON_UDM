Sunaookami Shiroko

Sunaookami Shiroko (砂狼シロコ) is one of the students studying in Abydos High School who wields an Assault Rifle, and She is a member of the Countermeasures Committee and is one of the deuteragonists

INTRODUCTION

The sporty field captain of the Abydos Foreclosure Task Force.
A girl of few words and almost no facial expression, Shiroko gives the expression of being frigid and emotionless. In truth, she is more devoted to Abydos High School than anything else. If it's for the sake of revitalizing the academy, she'll do what it takes and sometimes propose some outlandish ideas herself.

Personality

Shiroko is a soft-spoken girl, who deeply cares for her friends, to the extent that she would do anything to help her friends towards their goal of revitalizing the school. Though she is generally calm and reserved, she hyperfocuses on certain interests and can sometimes be oblivious to how others would react to them.
She often bikes and walks far more than regular people can keep up, thinking distances of 10s of kilometers are average for her. She fantasizes about robbing banks and going on survival fishing trips, and being unable to do either of these things causes her to pout and act with remarkable petulance for how calm she usually is.

Shiroko is willing to fight Hina the moment she laid eyes on her despite the head prefect's reputation, stopping only at Ayane's begging. She requires people like Sensei and Ayane to hold her back and keep her sensible.

She also instinctively didn't like Wakamo for acting clingy towards Sensei, seeing how surprised Wakamo was at Sensei's presence and immediately filling her with lead in jealousy.

Appearance


Her grey hair is styled in a shoulder-length long bob cut. It is accompanied with a pair of wolf ears. The insides of her wolf ears are originally depicted as black in Hwansang's official full body portrait, but are also often shown simply as grey or dark grey. Prominent white animal ear fluff protrudes from both of her wolf ears. Below her left wolf ear, she sports a cyan, inverted cross-shaped hairpin. Often covered by her hair, she also has a pair of humanoid ears; she has a singular, cyan stud ear piercing on her right one. Her eyes have mismatched, slitted pupils - the left one being white. Shiroko's halo is cyan ring-shaped with four lines marked on its cardinal directions, with a blue circle inside. It resembles a simple gun crosshair. |

In her young version, She has short, nape-length hair, Everything else seems unchanged; the cross hair clip and wolf ears remain the same.

Uniform

She wears the standard Abydos High School uniform: a tucked, white dress shirt with a left breast pocket faintly embroidered with the school's logo and name, a cyan scarf with dark stripes, a cyan necktie with a navy blue cross mark at its blade, a navy blue blazer with silver buttons and white accents, a lime-green and cyan Abydos badge hanging off the left side of the blazer.

She wears a pleated, dark grey and black skirt with a plaid pattern with white accents and wears white panties. She wears a single lime-green fingerless cycling glove on her left hand in her standard appearance.

She carries a Japanese-style black shoulder bag with cyan and silver accents and a triangular designer logo, with three keychains: a lime green cat winking and smiling, a silver cross or ankh, and a pink heart. She wears knee-high socks with triangular logos, similar to the ones on her bag, near the cuffs of the socks. Her running shoes are black with white and lime green accents, cyan shoe laces, and lime green soles. She also had a blue mask with the number two on her forehead and exposed wolf ears, but she usually kept it in her schoolbag when she wasn't robbing a bank.

In her younger version, she was wearing a navy blue blazer button fastening, was wearing a white shirt underneath, a blue tie, and a blue and black striped scarf that Hoshino given her. The dress remains unchanged. It has the Abydos badge, but no gloves, The shoes have some additional green details, The calf-length sock. She wore a Large backpack containing a gun on one shoulder.

Gym ver: Serika-style uniform: She was wearing a black sports top with white sleeves and black trim. The sleeves, hem, and collar were blue. She also wore black underwear with white trim on the sides, and two white socks with black horizontal stripes, The black and white shoes have a white sole in the middle cyan in color.

Firearm

She uses a white SIG SG 550 assault rifle customized with a lower receiver from an M4 as well as a black suppressor and vertical grip. The charging handle and fire selector switch is painted cyan. It is equipped with a SUSAT L9A1 sight with a blue lens. An Abydos emblem is stamped on the mag well. The phrase "ABYDOS 550" is written on the suppressor.
For her EX skill she deploys a white flying drone with black and cyan accents, equipped with two rotors and two missile pods.
