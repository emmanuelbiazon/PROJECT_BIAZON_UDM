Hoshino

She is the president of the Abydos Student Council, the chairwoman of the Countermeasures Committee, and one of the deuteragonists of Volume 1.

INTRODUCTION

Takanashi Hoshino is the president of the Abydos Student Council, the only registered third-year of Abydos High School, and the current chairwoman for the Countermeasures Commitee.

Personality

Speaking like a senile aunt, she enjoys playing around and napping more than work. Because of this, she usually gets scolded by the rest of the Committee. But, once the mission starts, she protects her comrades by fighting valiantly on the front lines. Since accepting the title of student council president, she has gradually brightened up. Perhaps this is the real Hoshino. Young: Guarded by a traumatic past, Hoshino’s younger self is characterized as an extremely serious, stern, and unapproachable individual. She is highly straightforward, blunt, and harbors a grumpy temperament, showing absolutely zero tolerance for slackers or nonsense. Deeply scarred and fiercely independent, she possesses an ironclad, stubborn will; she fiercely refuses to rely on others or show even a fraction of vulnerability, believing that exposing weakness invites destruction. She operates with a rigid militaristic mindset, often being cynical and cold, and is fully willing to resort to overwhelming tactical violence to eliminate threats and solve problems immediately, making both allies and enemies deeply wary of her presence.

Appearance

She has pink hair reaching to her legs in the back with a cowlick in the front. She has heterochromia of blue and orange eyes. She has a small cute little fang. Young: Hoshino features a noticeably shorter hairstyle than her current self, with her vibrant pastel-pink hair cut in a short bob that frames her face, accentuated by a prominent, twitching cowlick (ahoge) on top. Her hair is tied into two small, low-slung twin-tails near her ears, secured with simple black hair ties instead of her signature whale hairpins. While she possesses the same distinct heterochromia eyes—a bright sky-blue left eye and a golden-yellow right eye—her gaze is completely devoid of its usual sleepy warmth. Instead, her eyes appear dull and sharp, constantly exuding a cold, piercing, and intensely serious aura that keeps everyone at a distance.
Hoshino's halo has a pink eye-like circle with an outer line curved around and ends with a strip line.

Uniform

Hoshino wears the Abydos school uniform in a relaxed, casual style. She omits the standard blazer entirely, wearing a loose white collared dress shirt with the top buttons left undone and her vibrant cyan-blue tie loosely fastened around her neck. Her Abydos student identification access badge is clipped near her shirt pocket on the left chest. Across her torso, she wears a distinct black tactical shoulder harness with adjustment buckles, paired with a matching black waist belt. Her shirt is neatly tucked into a dark gray pleated plaid skirt featuring a subtle grid pattern. Her hands are covered with fingerless black tactical gloves. For her footwear, she wears an asymmetrical pair of white socks, with the left side being a standard ribbed sock and the right side being a loose ankle sock, completed with navy blue high-top sneakers with white laces and soles.
Gym: She was wearing a white top with light blue trim, the collar pulled to the right revealing her collarbone. Tight-fitting black athletic short. Wear a small white cloth on she right hand.

Young: For her young version, Hoshino wears a neat and strictly fastened Abydos school uniform. It consists of a crisp white collared dress shirt with a vibrant cyan-blue tie properly tightened around her neck. Over her shirt, she wears a heavily detailed black tactical bulletproof vest featuring multiple utility pockets, a prominent pistol holster mounted in the middle of the chest, and a red-and-white striped insignia tag on the left chest. Her right upper arm is fitted with a wide black armband displaying the Abydos logo. She pairs this with a dark gray pleated plaid skirt with a subtle grid pattern. Her hands are covered with fingerless black tactical gloves. On her legs, she wears a symmetrical pair of plain white knee-high socks, completed with sturdy black tactical boots

Firearm

She uses a two-tone Beretta 1301 Tactical semi-automatic shotgun with a black vent ribbed 21" Comp barrel, bolt carrier, and tubular magazine with a +3 Nordic Extension Tube. The rest of the firearm is painted white with pink accents. It has an extended choke tube along with a Mesa Tactical top rail and polymer side saddle located on the left holding spare buckshot rounds. The Abydos insignia is imprinted on the cheek rest.
For her EX-Skill she deploys the "Iron Horus". A collapsible ballistic shield that houses several more buckshot rounds and a backup Beretta 92FS pistol.
