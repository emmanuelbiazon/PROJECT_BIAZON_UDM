Izayoi Nonomi

INTRODUCTION

Izayoi Nonomi (十六夜ノノミ) is one of the students studying in Abydos High School who wields a Machine Gun. She is a member of the Countermeasures Committee. Although she is the daughter of a very rich family, and most of the snacks for the Council come out from her allowance.

Personality

She has an emotional and gentle personality, and serves as a mental support for the Committee, many of whom have extreme personalities.

Appearance

She has pale blonde, long hair reaching to her waist with side bun on her left side. She has emerald green eyes, and Nonomi's halo has a neon green circle with another smaller circle inside and has 2 earlaps

Uniform

She dons the Abydos uniform but forgoes the tie and blazer and instead wears a white shirt under a beige cardigan with black piping loosely off her shoulders. The Abydos access badge hangs around her neck on a lanyard, grey skirt, a pair of black socks and white trainers
Her hands are covered with black tracking gloves and an Abydos access badge wrapped around her neck.

Item: She has a green, hooded bank robber's hat with the number three on it.

For her young version, Nonomi wears a neat and formal school uniform. It consists of a dark navy blue blazer with thin light-blue piping along the lapels and pockets, worn over a black vest, a white collared dress shirt, and a vibrant cyan-blue tie. She pairs this with a dark gray pleated plaid skirt
featuring subtle grid patterns, solid black tights, and classic gray loafer shoes. Her long ash-blonde hair is styled with a side bun on the left, and she retains her bright yellow halo above her head. She is also seen carrying a large, heavy black equipment bag slung over her shoulder, presumably containing her minigun.

Firearm

She uses a black and white M134 "Minigun" with hints of green painted in some parts of the gun. A 'chainsaw grip' is attached above the receiver for hip-fire portability. A box magazine is attached below the weapon although no feed chute is present connecting the ammunition box to the feeder-delinker. The Abydos insignia is imprinted on the housing.
